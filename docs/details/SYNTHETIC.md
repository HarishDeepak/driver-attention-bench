# Synthetic arm (Blender + MPFB2)

A rendered driver performs the same scripted events as `docs/RECORDING.md`, so every event has an exact label and the existing pipeline can be scored on real vs synthetic clips with the same metrics (domain-gap measurement).

## Install (once, outside the repo)

| What | Where | Version |
|---|---|---|
| Blender (portable zip, download.blender.org) | `C:\blender\blender-4.5.9-windows-x64` | 4.5.9 LTS |
| MPFB2 extension (extensions.blender.org), installed with `blender -b --command extension install-file -r user_default --enable mpfb.zip` | `%APPDATA%\Blender Foundation\Blender\4.5\extensions\user_default\mpfb` | 2.0.18 |
| MakeHuman asset packs (files.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/<pack>/<pack>_cc0.zip), unzipped into `...\extensions\.user\user_default\mpfb\data` | system assets, skins01, skins02, eyebrows01, eyelashes01 (not used), hair01 | CC0 |

No accounts or forms were needed.

## Run

```bash
bash tools/blender/render_all.sh            # 6 clips, 3 Blender processes in parallel -> data/synthetic/*.mp4 + labels.csv
# one clip / one test frame:
blender -b -P tools/blender/make_driver.py -- --clip synth03_owl --seed 3 [--acc none|sunglasses|mask] [--still 5.0]
wsl bash -lc 'cd /mnt/c/<path-to>/driver-attention-bench && bash run_suite.sh scenarios/synthetic.toml'   # -> out/synthetic/
```

`data/synthetic/` is in `.gitignore` (renders and labels are regenerated by the script; the first render compiles EEVEE shaders for about 2 minutes, later runs use the cache).

## How it is generated (`tools/blender/make_driver.py`)

- **Human**: `--seed` drives sex, race (skin texture + race-matched face targets), age (25 or about 45 y), muscle/weight/height/proportions, eye colour, eyebrows, hair. Seeds 1, 2, 3 are a Caucasian man, a Caucasian woman and an African man. Rig = MPFB2 `default` skeleton, clothes = system casual suit.
- **Eyes closed**: MakeHuman face units `eye-left-closure` / `eye-right-closure` as shape keys (value 0 = open, 1 = closed). Blink = 0.2 s, closures ramp in 0.12 s.
- **Head**: yaw/pitch split over `neck01..03` and `head` bones (15/15/20/50 %). **Eyes only**: `eye.L` / `eye.R` bones, head fixed.
- **Accessories**: sunglasses = two flat ellipsoids, bridge, brow bar and arms; mask = a grid shrink-wrapped to the lower face. Both are plain meshes bound to the `head` bone, no texture.
- **Camera / scene**: 640x480, 25 fps, EEVEE, 12 samples, 30 mm lens, 0.6 m from the eyes, 25 deg to the driver's right of straight ahead, 8 deg below eye level (`--cam-az --cam-dist --cam-el`). Dark cabin wall and headrest, one key area light plus a weak fill, plain RGB (use the existing `ir` variant for an IR look).
- **Clips** (same event order as RECORDING.md; the first 3.5 s are 3 s calibration plus 0.5 s margin):

| Clip | Human | Events after calibration |
|---|---|---|
| `synth01_microsleep` | 1 | 3 blinks, closed 2 s, open 4 s, closed 2 s, open 3 s |
| `synth02_sleep` | 2 | closed 5 s, open 4 s, closed 4 s, open 3 s |
| `synth03_owl` | 3 | head right 50 deg 4 s, back 4 s, head left 40 deg + down 25 deg 4 s, back 3 s, 1 s glance right, back 3 s |
| `synth04_lizard` | 1 | eyes only right 42 deg 4 s, back 4 s, eyes only down 38 deg 4 s, back 3 s |
| `synth05_sunglasses` | 2 + sunglasses | closed 2 s, open 4 s, closed 5 s, open 4 s, head right 4 s, back 3 s |
| `synth06_mask` | 3 + mask | same as 05 |

## Labels (exact)

`data/synthetic/labels.csv` has the same columns as the real labels (`clip,kind,t_start,t_end`). Times come from the animation function sampled at 1 ms, not from frames: `closed` = eyelid closure at least 0.95, `away` = head plus eye angle more than 30 deg from forward. Blinks and the 1 s glance are not labelled (only closed >= 1 s and away >= 2 s are written), as in RECORDING.md. The sunglasses clip keeps the `closed` labels although the eyes are hidden behind the lenses (same as the real clip).

## Limitations

- Synthetic eyelids are a shape-key blend (no eyelash geometry, no lid thickness or tear film, eyes do not roll up when closing); skin is a diffuse texture without pores or subsurface detail; hair is a rigid cap.
- Head motion is a perfectly smooth smoothstep ramp with no micro-motion, no breathing, no speech; blinks are identical in shape.
- Sunglasses and mask are plain untextured meshes, the mask does not deform with the jaw.
- One camera pose, one light setup, no windscreen, glare or IR sensor model; 3 identities only. A small score gap does not prove the pipeline transfers to real cabins.
- The "real" arm is also tiny (one person, one phone), so the comparison shows direction, not a statistic.

## Result (2026-10-20, variant `clean`, no CLAHE, `out/synthetic/`)

MediaPipe found the face in 3343 of 3447 frames (97 %); all misses are the 50 deg head turn in `synth03_owl` (face 523/627).

| Variant | Hits | Expected | Rate (Wilson 95 %) | Median abs timing error | False alerts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| clean | 15 | 18 | 0.83 (0.61-0.94) | 0.04 s | 3 |

- The 3 misses are all `synth05_sunglasses` eye-closure events: the blendshapes see no eyes behind the dark lenses (629/679 frames `eyes_unknown`). Head turn with sunglasses is a hit.
- The 3 false alerts are `long_distraction` while the eyes are closed (`synth02_sleep` x2, `synth06_mask` x1): the pipeline reads closed eyes as gaze off the road.
- The 25 deg off-axis camera gave enough detection, so no frontal-camera retry was run.
